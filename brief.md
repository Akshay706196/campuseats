# CampusEats Brief

## What

CampusEats is a digital food service system designed for a college campus. Instead of students visiting different food counters to check what is available, they can use one system to explore food options and send their orders. The system can also help food vendors keep track of incoming orders and update their availability.

The main idea of CampusEats is to make the campus food ordering process more convenient for students and easier to manage for food providers.

## Who

CampusEats has three main types of users:

- **Students:** Browse food choices, check prices, select items, place orders, and see the progress of their orders.
- **Food Vendors:** Manage their food list, update item availability, receive orders, and change the status of an order.
- **Administrator:** Maintains the system, manages users and vendors, and monitors the overall food service.
